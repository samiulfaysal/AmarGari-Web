AmarGari

Smart Vehicle Maintenance, Fuel & BRTA Document Tracker

AmarGari helps motorcycle and car owners in Bangladesh log fuel fill-ups, calculate real-world mileage (km/L), track servicing intervals, and receive timely reminders before BRTA tax token, fitness, insurance, and driving license expiries.

Fuel & Mileage Analytics

Record petrol, octane, diesel, or CNG refills with odometer readings to automatically calculate fuel efficiency and running cost per kilometer.

Maintenance & Service Logs

Keep complete service histories for engine oil changes, tire replacements, battery checks, and periodic repairs across multiple vehicles.

BRTA Document Reminders

Track expiration dates for Registration, Tax Token, Fitness Certificate, Route Permit, and Driving License with local notifications.

Offline-First with Cloud Backup

Works completely offline on your Android device, with optional one-tap backup and restore to your personal Google Drive.

How AmarGari Uses Google User Data

AmarGari stores all vehicle logs locally on your device by default. Connecting your Google account is strictly optional and is used solely for personal cloud backup and restore:

  • Google Sign-In (Profile & Email): Used only to authenticate your account and display your signed-in status inside the app's Backup settings.
  • Google Drive App Data (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): Used exclusively to save and restore AmarGari backup files inside a hidden, app-specific folder in your personal Google Drive.
  • Strict Isolation: AmarGari cannot view, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive, and your vehicle data is never sold or shared with third parties.
