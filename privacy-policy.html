Privacy Policy — AmarGari: Bike & Car Tracker

Package: com.nlab.amargari • Developer: NLab • Effective Date: November 2026

NLab built AmarGari: Bike & Car Tracker as an offline-first utility application. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use our app.

1. Local Offline Storage

All vehicle profiles, fuel logs, engine oil service histories, and BRTA document expiry dates you enter are stored locally on your device using an internal SQLite database. We do not operate external servers that collect or store your personal vehicle logs.

2. Optional Google Drive Cloud Backup

AmarGari provides an optional backup and restore feature powered by the Google Drive API (App Data Folder scope). If you choose to sign in with your Google Account:

  • Your backup file is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS and stored exclusively inside your own Google Drive's hidden application folder.
  • AmarGari only accesses files created by AmarGari itself and cannot view, modify, or delete any of your other Google Drive files.
  • You can disconnect your Google Account or delete your app backup anytime from the app settings or your Google Account permissions page.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the app free, AmarGari displays banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, crash logs, diagnostics, and app interaction data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Permissions Used

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used solely to deliver offline local reminders 30 days and 7 days before your BRTA documents (Tax Token, Fitness, Insurance, Driving License) expire.
  • Internet & Network State: Used to load AdMob banners and perform user-initiated Google Drive backups.

5. Children's Privacy

AmarGari is intended for vehicle owners and licensed drivers and is not directed to children under the age of 13.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or data deletion inquiries regarding AmarGari, contact us at support@nlab.click or visit https://amargari.nlab.click.
